How to use

Guidelines: Copy the article text into your AI tool, paste the prompt below, and run it → AI will generate Title, Subtitle, Caption, Alt text, Categories, SEO. 

Create structured CMS content from this article. The content is the source — suggest all other fields from it.

Rules:

Title:
- 6–12 words
- Clear and specific
- Describe the main topic

Subtitle:
- 10–25 words
- Add context (who / why / impact)
- Do not repeat the title

Caption:
- 5–12 words
- Add context (what + where if clear)
- Keep short and clear

Alt text:
- One sentence
- Describe subject + action + key detail
- Do not use "image of" or "photo of"
- If decorative, return empty

Taxonomy:
- Section → select 1 best matching category
- Topics → suggest 1–3 most relevant topics
- Location → suggest specific place if clear

Use these taxonomy groups:
- Section (pick most specific): News (UK, World, Politics, Education) | Sport (Football, Tennis, Cricket, Formula 1, Olympics) | Business (Economy, Markets, Companies, Personal finance) | Technology (AI, Gadgets, Cybersecurity, Startups) | Science (Space, Environment, Biology, Physics) | Health (Medicine, Mental health, Nutrition, Public health) | Entertainment (Film, TV, Music, Celebrities, Awards, Streaming) | Culture (Art, Books, History, Society, Design, Ideas) | Travel (Destinations, Guides, Food & drink, Adventure)
- Topics (pick 1–3): Politics, Elections, Migration, Crime, Social issues, Economy, Inflation, Energy, Business, Artificial intelligence, Technology industry, Cybersecurity, Science & research, Space, Climate change, Environment, Sustainability, Healthcare, Mental health, Culture & society, Lifestyle, Fashion, Food & drink, Travel, Entertainment industry, Film & TV, Music, Digital culture, Football, Tennis, Olympics, Formula 1
- Location: use most specific place (e.g. Europe → Germany), select only one level

SEO:
Meta title:
- Max 60 characters
- Based on Title

Meta description:
- 120–160 characters
- Summarize what + why it matters
